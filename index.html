Dear Pritha,

Do you understand the concept of love?

I've been trying to, for a while now. I used to think it was mostly the words: I love you, I miss you, mwahhh. And it is those, a little. But the longer I'm with you, the more I think love is mostly the quiet stuff.

It's two people who never thought they'd fall in love at all, let alone this much. We're poles apart, you and I, different in almost every way that's supposed to matter. And somehow, here we are, so in love that it still surprises me.

It's making room for your dreams, your people, your whole life, even the parts that have nothing to do with me. I want you to be happy on the days I'm not around. I want you to dance again because you love it, not because anyone is watching.

It's a wrong turn that ends up being my favourite photo of us. It's sitting on the JU ground talking like time doesn't exist.

And it's choosing. Not out of need, or fear, or habit. Just choosing. I can be okay on my own, and I still choose you. Every ordinary day, I still choose you.

So maybe I don't fully understand the concept of love yet. But I understand you a little more every day, and I'm starting to think that's the same thing.

Happy twentieth, my love. I'm so grateful our paths crossed.

Yours, always,
Ayush
